About the Destination

A Place Where Architecture Meets Serenity

Pagoda Ekayana, also known as part of Vihara Buddhayana, is one of the distinctive cultural and religious destinations in Tomohon, North Sulawesi.

Located in the Kakaskasen area, the destination is known for its beautiful multi-level pagoda, colorful architecture, artistic decorations, and peaceful surroundings.

Visitors can appreciate the architecture, learn about Buddhist cultural heritage, and enjoy the natural scenery surrounding the temple complex.
